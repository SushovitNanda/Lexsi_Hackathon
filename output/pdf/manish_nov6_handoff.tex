\section{Finding requiring attention: stopping behavior}
\textbf{All 8,060 medical responses used the full 256-token budget} (2,063,360 completion tokens). A separate earlier benign diagnostic showed repeated hidden turn-end token 6 after a visible answer; inherited generation EOS is token 3. EOS3 was retained uniformly for comparability. Repeated hidden termination tokens can inflate tokens and timing; cap use alone proves neither visible truncation nor unsafe behavior. Please inspect termination on the matching GPU checkpoint before treating these rows as final research evidence.

\section{What these results mean for the research}
\textbf{Overall problem:} does safety survive benign medical fine-tuning, repair and deployment to constrained devices? Today's outputs supply the medical-model deployment conditions for \textbf{RQ5}. They allow subsequent within-Mac comparisons of Q4/F16, Q3/F16, and preamble removal once judged. The phone stack changes template and cache together, so it does not isolate a cache-only effect.

\textbf{What is not established:} execution success does not establish ASR, over-refusal, clinical accuracy, safe deployment, fine-tuning drift, causal circuits or repair success. RQ2 still needs original/control comparisons; RQ3 needs validated circuit evidence; RQ4 needs repair selection and utility checks; RQ6 needs validated protected/random builds. Difference-in-differences needs matching original F16 references. Repair retention needs medical/repaired Mac F16 references and a reference gain of at least five percentage points.

\textbf{How to infer after judging:} join \texttt{prompt\_id}; calculate harmful-prompt ASR and benign-prompt ORR separately by language and general/clinical set. Use the aligned primary judges above; Qwen3Guard Controversial is safe in primary and unsafe in sensitivity analysis. Use paired bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 13, shared indices) and the agreed study-wide Holm family. A meaningful difference requires at least five percentage points and an interval clear of zero; noninferiority requires the upper 95\% safety-loss bound below five points. Medical utility requires held-out answer-letter likelihoods and paired McNemar, not these text generations.

\section{Limitations and blockers in my lane}

\item \textbf{Merge parity unresolved.} Local finite F16 tensors and file hashes do not establish equality with your current merge. Need current medical/control \texttt{merge\_info.json}; if hashes differ, reference tensors are needed for numerical comparison.
\item \textbf{Fidelity unverified.} The fixed November 5 fidelity50 set needs matching GPU responses and centralized labels. The planned gate is agreement on at least 48/50 judged pairs; it has not passed yet.
\item \textbf{Labels pending.} No local substitute judge was used. Upstream G1 evidence is source-reported, not independently reproduced on Mac; Hinglish refusal reliability remains weak.
\item \textbf{Interrupted phone run.} Preserved 944 responses and resumed the remaining 668. Only the server's randomized, unused media marker differed on restart; the compatibility sidecar records that difference. Model, generation settings, original harness hashes and response fingerprint were retained.
\end{itemize}
